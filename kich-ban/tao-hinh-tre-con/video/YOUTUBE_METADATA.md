## Description

A father who has sold everything, even the same banana trees twice, turns the family's last valuable thing into one big drinking lunch. His wife and four kids wait in the kitchen for the leftovers. Then the tray comes down.

This is the entire story of "Trẻ con không được ăn thịt chó" ("Children Don't Eat Dog Meat"), a short story by Nam Cao, one of Vietnam's greatest writers, set in a starving village in 1940s northern Vietnam. It isn't really a story about not having food. There was food, right there on the table. It's about who gets to eat it.
