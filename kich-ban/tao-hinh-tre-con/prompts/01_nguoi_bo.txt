CHARACTER: the father, a lazy, greedy Vietnamese peasant man around 40, thin but with a slight pot belly, smug half-lidded eyes, a sly self-satisfied grin, cheeks a little red from rice liquor, short messy black hair, a few days of stubble. Faded indigo-brown short peasant shirt (áo cánh) half unbuttoned and patched, loose dark brown trousers rolled to mid-calf, barefoot. Holding a small clay bottle of rice liquor in one hand. He looks like a man who has never worked a full day in his life.
