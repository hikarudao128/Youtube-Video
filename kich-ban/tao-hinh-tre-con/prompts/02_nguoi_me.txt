CHARACTER: the mother (called "thị"), a very thin, exhausted Vietnamese peasant woman in her mid 30s, sunken cheeks, tired sad eyebrows, gentle mouth pressed shut, black hair wrapped in a dark brown headscarf (khăn vấn), worn dark-brown blouse (áo cánh) with several patches, black long skirt (váy đụp) to the shins, barefoot. Carrying an empty woven bamboo market basket on one arm. Small, bent shoulders, but she is the one keeping the family alive.
