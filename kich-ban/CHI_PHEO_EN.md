A gentle farmhand found as a baby in an abandoned brick kiln becomes the most feared man in his village — and it all comes down to a rich landlord, one silver coin and one pot of scallion porridge. This is the entire story of *Chí Phèo*, Nam Cao's 1941 classic of Vietnamese literature, told from the first curse to the last callback. Why did the "demon of Vũ Đại" kill the man who made him, the very day he tried to become decent again?
