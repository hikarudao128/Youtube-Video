CHARACTER: Bá Kiến, a rich, cunning old village chief in his 60s: plump round body, pale well-fed skin, sly smiling closed crescent eyes and raised eyebrows, thin grey mustache and small grey chin tuft, a small double chin, wearing a black pleated Vietnamese turban (khăn xếp), a long black silk tunic robe (áo the) reaching below the knees fastened at the right shoulder with small gold buttons, white silk trousers, dark wooden clogs, holding a folded cream paper fan in one hand and a gold ring on the other hand. Looks friendly on the surface but scheming.
