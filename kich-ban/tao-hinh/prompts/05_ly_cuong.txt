CHARACTER: Lý Cường, the arrogant son of a rich village chief, about 30: tall and thin with a long narrow face, pale skin, narrow eyes, angry sharp eyebrows, sneering lopsided mouth, black pleated Vietnamese turban (khăn xếp), a long dark slate-blue silk tunic robe (áo the) reaching below the knees, white trousers, black cloth shoes, holding a thin rattan cane.
