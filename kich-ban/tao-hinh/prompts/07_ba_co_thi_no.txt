CHARACTER: the old aunt of Thị Nở, a sharp-tongued old village woman in her 60s: thin, slightly hunched back, wrinkled tanned face, narrow suspicious eyes, angry eyebrows, a dark red-stained mouth from chewing betel nut, a few grey hairs showing under a dark brown headscarf tied with a point on the forehead (khăn mỏ quạ), a dark brown short peasant blouse with cloth buttons, a long black skirt, barefoot (tan oval bare feet), holding a round woven bamboo hand fan (quạt nan).
