CHARACTER: Bà Ba, the young beautiful third wife of a rich village chief, about 25: pale skin, thin arched eyebrows, small eyelashes, small red lips from chewing betel, black hair under a dark rolled turban headwrap (khăn vấn) wrapped around the head like a ring, a mustard-yellow silk short blouse (áo cánh lụa) with a peach-pink camisole (yếm) visible at the V neckline, long black silk trousers, light wooden clogs, holding a small red folding fan. Haughty, languid expression.
